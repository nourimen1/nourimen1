### 🌟 Featured Project

#### 🎵 MiMotion — Technology Meets Music

MiMotion is an innovative project that explores the intersection of music, human interaction and intelligent technology.

Initially developed as my final-year engineering project, MiMotion combines a smart wearable musical glove with a digital learning platform.

The project explores:
- Wearable technology and gesture recognition.
- Embedded systems and wireless communication.
- Interactive music learning.
- Intelligent musical interaction.

**My vision:** Evolve MiMotion into a comprehensive technology platform for musicians, combining AI, voice recognition, computer vision, IoT and immersive experiences.

🔗 [Explore MiMotion](https://mimotion.netlify.app/)
